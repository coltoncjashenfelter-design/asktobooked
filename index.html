The new layer of local discovery

AI visibility, explained simply

Homeowners are beginning to ask conversational tools the same questions they once typed into a search bar.

Those tools do not judge the quality of your work firsthand. They work from the information they can find, interpret, compare, and trust. That means two equally capable businesses can receive very different exposure.

asktobooked turns that uncertainty into a repeatable process: ask the real questions, record the answers, find the clearest information gaps, correct them, and recheck.

Common questions

Is this just another name for SEO?
No. Traditional local search still matters, and much of the groundwork overlaps. The audit specifically tests how conversational and AI-generated answers represent and recommend your business.
Can anyone guarantee that my business will be recommended?
No. Results can change by platform, location, wording, personalization, and time. The goal is to strengthen the information available about your business and measure what changes.
Where should I begin?
