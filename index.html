What's actually going wrong

This has nothing to do with how good you are at the job.

These tools have never seen a thing you've built or repaired. They go off whatever they can find written about you online. Which means two shops doing identical work can end up in completely different positions.

There are only one or two slots

Google hands you ten blue links to compete for. ChatGPT usually names one or two companies and stops talking. Coming fourth used to be perfectly good business. Now it's the same as not being there.

Your details don't match up

Your site says you close at six. Your Google profile says five. Some directory still lists the number you dropped two years ago. None of that matters much to a person. To a machine deciding whether to vouch for you, it's a reason to pick someone else.

Nothing tells you it's happening

No missed call. No form someone abandoned halfway. No number in an analytics dashboard going the wrong way. You just have a slower month than you expected and no real idea why.

What we won't tell you

Nobody can guarantee your business a spot in a system that regenerates its answers every time it's asked. Results shift by platform, wording, location, and time. Anyone promising otherwise is selling something.

What you can do is tidy up what's out there about you, fix the bits that contradict each other, and check again next month. Do that consistently and you'll be ahead of most of your competition, who aren't doing any of it.
