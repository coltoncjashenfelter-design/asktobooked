What's actually going wrong

It isn't your work. It's what the machines can read about your work.

AI tools don't visit your jobsites. They compare whatever information they can find, interpret, and trust — so two equally good companies can get very different exposure.

One question, a few names

A search page shows ten links. An AI answer often gives one to three businesses. Being on page one of Google no longer means being in the answer.

Your information disagrees with itself

Hours on your site, different hours on your profile, an old number in a directory. Inconsistency reads as uncertainty, and uncertainty doesn't get recommended.

You can't see it happening

There's no missed-call log for this. No notification, no bounce rate, no lost quote. The jobs simply never reach you, and nothing tells you why.
