Common questions

Do you guarantee first place in ChatGPT?
No — and be wary of anyone who does. We help you close clarity gaps your competitors haven't. The goal is visibility habits, not a magic ranking.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. The guide uses tools you already have — your Google Business Profile, website builder, and directories. Most fixes take minutes, not a developer.

The guide teaches you to run the audit yourself. Consulting means we run it for you, compare your competitors, and hand you a prioritized fix list — plus optional monthly tracking.

Each audit is personalized — your trade, your city, your competitors. We cap intake so every client gets real attention, not a templated report.
Why isn't my Google ranking enough?
Research found fewer than half of businesses ranking well in ordinary local search also showed up in AI answers. They're two separate contests.
