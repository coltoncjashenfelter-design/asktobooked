One thing I should be straight about

Nobody can promise you a fixed spot in something that writes a new answer every time it's asked. Anyone selling you a guaranteed ranking is either confused or lying.

This isn't a score you hit once and forget about. It's a handful of things worth looking at every month, and the businesses that stay visible are usually just the ones that bother.

Common questions

Do you guarantee first place in ChatGPT?
No, and I'd be careful with anyone who says otherwise. What the guide does is close the gaps your competitors have left open, which is usually enough.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. Everything runs through your Google profile, whatever you built your website with, and your directory listings. Most of the fixes take a few minutes each.
How is consulting different from the guide?
The guide teaches you to run this yourself. With consulting we run it for you, do the fixing, and show you what changed. Monthly tracking is optional on top.

Why isn't my Google ranking enough?
Ranking well on Google doesn't reliably carry over. Plenty of businesses sitting in the local pack don't get mentioned by AI tools at all. They're two different competitions.
