Refund Policy

Effective July 22, 2026

Every purchase of Who Shows Up First? comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you work through the guide and don't feel it was worth it, you get your money back. No forms, no hoops, no "let me speak to my manager."

How to request a refund

  • Email contact@asktobooked.com within 30 days of your purchase.
  • Send it from — or mention — the email address you used at checkout, so we can find your order.
  • That's it. You don't need to give a reason. If you're willing to share what didn't land, we'd like to know so the guide gets better — but it's optional and never a condition.

What happens next

We refund the full purchase price to your original payment method through Stripe, normally within one business day of your email. Depending on your bank, the money typically appears in 5–10 business days. You'll get a confirmation email from us when it's issued.

The fine print (kept short)

  • The 30-day window starts on the date of purchase.
  • After a refund, your license to use the guide ends — we ask that you delete your copy.
  • Requests after 30 days aren't covered by the guarantee, but email us anyway; we'd rather hear from you than not.

Consulting

Consulting engagements are scoped and agreed by email before any payment, and any refund terms for consulting are set out in that agreement. This 30-day guarantee applies to the field guide.

Questions

Anything unclear, before or after buying: contact@asktobooked.com.
