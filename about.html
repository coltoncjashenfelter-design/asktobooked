About asktobooked

Doing good work shouldn't be the reason nobody's heard of you.

asktobooked started with a question nobody could answer for us: when an AI recommends one local business over another, what actually made the difference?

Why this exists

It started with a search for a mechanic

We needed someone to look at a car. Nothing dramatic, the kind of errand everybody runs a few times a year. So we did what most people do now and asked an AI tool who to take it to.

It came back with one name. No list, no options, no "here are a few places to consider." One shop, stated plainly, like the question had a correct answer.

That stuck with us. There were a dozen mechanics within a few miles, and we couldn't work out what made that one the answer. So we went and looked. Same star ratings. Same sort of review counts. Some had been in business longer. A couple were cheaper.

The difference wasn't quality. It was that one shop had written down what it did, where it did it, and when it was open, in a way a machine could actually read. Everybody else had the same information scattered across a website, a Google profile and three directories that all disagreed with each other. So the machine skipped them.

None of those owners knew it was happening. There is no notification for this. You do not get told that a customer asked a question and somebody else got named.

We started running the same test on other trades in other cities to see whether it held up, and it did, over and over. Plumbers, roofers, electricians. The businesses getting named were rarely the best ones. They were the legible ones.

That is the entire reason asktobooked exists. Most of what decides this is dull, mechanical and fixable in an afternoon once somebody shows you where to look. The guide shows you. The consulting means we do it for you.

How we approach the work

How we work

No jargon

You shouldn't need to learn a new vocabulary to understand what's being said about your own company.

Your business, not a template

A checklist written for everyone is worth very little. What matters is your trade, your city, and the specific companies turning up instead of you.

A short list, not a long one

Handing someone a hundred things to fix is the same as handing them nothing. We'd rather find the few that actually move the needle.

Check it again

These answers change. A screenshot from March tells you almost nothing in September.

Where we stop

We can't promise you a spot, and neither can anyone else

Nobody can guarantee your business a fixed position in a system that writes a fresh answer every time somebody asks. It shifts by platform, by how the question is phrased, by where the person is standing.

What we can do is make you easier to understand, clear up the things that contradict each other, get your site answering the questions customers actually ask, and then measure it again to see if it worked.
