Common questions

How is this different from the $29.99 guide?
The guide teaches you to run the audit yourself. Consulting means we run it for you, compare your competitors, and hand you a prioritized fix list — plus optional monthly tracking.

No — and be wary of anyone who does. AI answers regenerate constantly. What we deliver is a clear picture of where you stand and the highest-leverage fixes to close the gaps your competitors haven't.
What does it cost?
Pricing depends on scope — how many platforms, competitors, and locations we audit. It's confirmed in writing on your strategy call, before any payment. The call itself is free.
